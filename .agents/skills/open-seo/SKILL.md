# OpenSEO — AI Agent SEO Intelligence

Derived from [every-app/open-seo](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo).

## Capabilities

1. **Keyword Research & Demand**: Discover volume, intent, and keyword difficulty across organic search.
2. **Technical Site Audits**: Scan HTTP headers, canonical tags, `noindex` directives, robots.txt, XML sitemaps, and rendering issues.
3. **AI Visibility & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO)**: Track citations and answers generated by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.
4. **Competitor & Backlink Intelligence**: Map referring domains and content gap opportunities.

## Operational Directives for Agents

- **Prioritize 1 High-Impact Action**: Never overwhelm users with 50 generic recommendations; present the single most urgent "do-this-week" fix.
- **Evidence-First Verification**: Every claim must link to observed HTTP headers, DOM snapshots, or live search queries.
- **No False Guarantees**: Never promise exact rank positions or algorithmic traffic lifts.
